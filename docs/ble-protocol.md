# Pocket link contract (phone side)

The contract between 多多随身, the Passport firmware and the ambient channel, as this app
implements it. The Passport firmware implements the device side of sections 4–9; the channel
implements section 2. Change both ends together.

## 1. Audio

Opus, 16 kHz mono, 20 ms frames, complexity 0, VBR, DTX off while the button is held, FEC off.
One Opus packet is the unit on every hop: BLE AUDIO message, upload body, transcription request.

## 2. Voice-note upload

`POST /api/messages?room=<room>` on the channel. A voice note is a message: the sender ended it
and it is addressed to 多多.

- Headers: `Content-Type: application/vnd.ambient.opus-packets`, `Idempotency-Key: <uuid>`
  (the note's id, generated by the app, lowercase; it becomes the message id),
  `X-Voice-Source: passport | phone`.
- Body: `[u16 little-endian length][opus packet bytes]` per packet, in capture order.
- 202: `{"message_id", "at"}`: the channel holds the message. A repeated key returns the first
  receipt and never takes the message twice. Recognition and delivery follow as `/live` events
  (section 3).
- Errors (`{"error", "message_id"}`): 400 `bad_body` / `idempotency_key_required`, 413
  `body_too_large`, 415, 503 `perception_unavailable` / `upload_max_bytes_unset`. An error leaves
  nothing behind: the same key may be sent again. `/api/voice` and `/api/inject` answer 410
  `update_required`.
- Voice: the app retries a transport error or a 5xx with the same key until its deadline
  (Passport: `resultDeadline`; phone: `uploadDeadline`); other statuses are final.
- Typed: a transport error keeps the message queued and it is sent again, with the same key, when
  the connection returns. Any status other than 202, a 5xx included, is not retried
  automatically: the message is marked failed, and the user's manual resend reuses the same key.

## 3. Replies and the event stream

The app holds one `/live?room=…&since=<seq>` connection, `since` being the newest row it holds.
It carries:

- `row {seq, row}`: a room-log row was stored. Rows are written once; `seq` is the room's cursor.
  On connect the rows after `since` are replayed, then `ready`.
- `ready {seq, turn, messages, api}`: the end of the replay; `seq` is the cursor to keep, `turn`
  the open turn (its `message_ids` and phase) or null, `messages` every message not yet delivered
  or failed, with its state. `api` is the channel's client-API major, raised only by an
  incompatible change; the app needs 1 (`clientAPIMajor`).

Speech. `/live` takes `audio=0|1`, the surface's mouth switch for the connection's life; the app
sends `1` while ambient mode runs, or while 播放语音回复 is on and the app is in front (not while an
interruption such as a call holds ambient mode's audio session), and reconnects from the cursor when
that changes. An audio-on connection gets `audio_params` after `meta`. With ambient
on (`hello`, the capture seat) the room is in live mode: speech comes to the phone paced by its
`played`, and a press sends `interrupted`. With ambient off the room is in message mode: speech
arrives as fast as it is synthesized, the phone plays speeches in declaration order and sends no
report; a press stops and drops playback locally. A `speech` declaration never cuts audio already
received; `stop_audio` for the current speech, or for none, clears every buffered speech.

Read aloud: `POST /api/speech?room=` with JSON `{text}` (the text the bubble shows) streams the
synthesized packets back in the voice message framing. Errors: 413 `body_too_large`, 422
`nothing_to_speak`, 502 `synthesis_failed`, 503 `cerebellum_unavailable`; a body cut after the first
packet ends with a reset. Nothing is stored.

Upgrade signals. Each incompatible pairing names the side that must upgrade:

- App too old: the channel answers 410 `update_required` (the retired `/api/voice` and
  `/api/inject`, or a later retirement). The app shows 多多随身需要更新; tapping it opens the
  TestFlight sign-up form the README links. Testers keep TestFlight automatic updates on.
- Channel too old: a `ready` whose `api` is absent or below `clientAPIMajor`, or a 404 from
  `POST /api/messages`. The app shows 家里的 ambient 需要升级 and names the Upgrading section of
  ambient's `docs/deploy.md`. A later `ready` with a sufficient `api` clears it.
- Channel and cerebellum on different majors: `/api/state` carries `cerebellum_halt` with reason
  `protocol`. The app shows 小脑与 channel 版本不一致，需要两端同时升级 in its status and in place of
  the voice-unavailable notice.
- `message {id, state: "cleaned", text}`: a voice message's text as the brain receives it (raw
  recognition, or the judge-cleaned text where the channel has one).
- `message {id, state: "failed", reason}`: `empty_transcript`, `asr_failed`,
  `perception_unavailable`, or `brain_unreachable` (after the row was written).
- `turn {phase, message_ids, …}`: `received` is the delivery of the listed messages to the
  brain; `thinking`, `tool`, `speaking`, `done`, `idle` follow.
- `answer_final {message_ids, text, speech_id}` and its answer row (with the same `message_ids`;
  a cut answer's row has `full_text`). `duoduo_said` carries text deltas. The channel broadcasts
  `answer_final` before it persists the answer's row; the app's dedupe of live answers against
  their rows relies on that order.

Without a socket (Background App Refresh, a BLE wake) the app reads `GET /api/imlog?since=<seq>`:
`{entries, seq}`, the same rows. Correlation and the reply wait are in sections 8 and 9.

## 5. Security

LE Secure Connections with bonding and numeric comparison. The phone side has no pairing code:
the first subscribe to the encrypted TX characteristic makes iOS start pairing and show the
number; the user confirms it on the phone and with OK on the Passport. Re-pairing: forget the
device in iOS Settings › Bluetooth, re-pair on the Passport, then reopen the app's Device page.

## 6. PDU

`[type u8][flags u8][len u16 LE][payload]`, one PDU per ATT value. `flags bit0` = more fragments
follow. A message longer than one ATT value is split into consecutive PDUs of the same type.
Fragments of one message are consecutive: any other PDU arriving between them drops the partial
message. A PDU whose `len` differs from the value length is dropped.

- Fragment size: ATT value size minus 4. The phone uses
  `maximumWriteValueLength(for: .withoutResponse)` at send time; the device uses its
  negotiated MTU − 3.
- Max message size: **4096 bytes** in both directions (basis in `docs/constants.md`). A REPLY or RESULT text longer than that is cut at a character boundary
  with "…" for the device; the phone keeps the full text.

## 7. Messages

Device → phone:

| Type | Name | Payload |
|---|---|---|
| 0x01 | INFO | proto_major u8, proto_minor u8, firmware version (item 1), battery % u8, charging u8, preroll u8 |
| 0x02 | PRESS_START | press_id u16 |
| 0x03 | AUDIO | press_id u16, seq u16, one Opus packet |
| 0x04 | PRESS_END | press_id u16, packet count u16 |
| 0x05 | STATUS | battery % u8, charging u8 (on change) |
| 0x06 | KEEPALIVE | press_id u16 (only while waiting for a reply) |

Phone → device:

| Type | Name | Payload |
|---|---|---|
| 0x81 | RESULT | press_id u16, code u8 (0 transcribed, 1 empty, 2 asr_failed, 3 send_failed, 4 not_connected_to_server), UTF-8 text (the text the brain receives when code 0) |
| 0x82 | REPLY | reply_id u32, final u8, UTF-8 text (replace semantics: the device shows the latest full text for the id) |
| 0x83 | REPLY_DONE | reply_id u32 (the device stops its keepalive) |
| 0x84 | APP_STATE | state u8 (0 ok, 1 server unreachable, 2 protocol major mismatch), then language u8 (protocol 1.2, item 9) |
| 0x85 | WORK | phase u8, optional UTF-8 label (protocol 1.1) |

All integers are little-endian. INFO carries the protocol version: a different major is refused
with a visible error on both ends; minors are additive. The app speaks 1.2. A 1.0 device ignores
WORK as an unknown type; a 1.0 or 1.1 device ignores the language byte after APP_STATE's state
(its parser ignores bytes after the fixed fields). The device owns the tap threshold; the app treats a press of any length as
speech.

Payload details:

1. **INFO firmware version** is one length byte followed by the UTF-8 string (no NUL):
   `proto_major, proto_minor, fw_len, fw…, battery, charging, preroll`. Fields after `preroll`
   (later minors) are ignored.
2. **Charging** (INFO and STATUS): 0 no, 1 yes, 0xFF unknown. Any other value reads as unknown.
3. **AUDIO `seq`** starts at 0 for each press; a packet the device's link refused still consumes a
   seq, and PRESS_END's count is the number of seqs used. The phone counts the holes as gaps.
   `press_id` starts at a random value per device boot.
4. **APP_STATE 2** = protocol major mismatch; the device shows it.
5. **REPLY_DONE with `reply_id` 0** means "the phone stopped waiting without a reply" (the device
   stops its keepalive). The app sends it on `turn idle` of the press's message without the
   answer, when the message failed to reach the brain, or when the `/live` socket cannot be
   restored during a wait. Real reply ids are never 0.
6. **REPLY `reply_id`** comes from a per-phone counter: each new answer takes the last id plus
   one, but never less than the phone's current Unix time in seconds (skipping 0). The counter
   survives relaunches; the time floor keeps ids above every earlier id after a reinstall, which
   empties the counter (the Passport stores and alerts once per id, so a reused id would be shown
   silently and not stored). Ids are unique and increase in the order answers were sent; they are
   not consecutive. Partial texts of one utterance share the id (`final` 0); the answer ends
   with `final` 1 and REPLY_DONE. An answer sent again (link ready, or closing a wait with an
   answer the device already has) keeps the id it was first sent with.
7. **KEEPALIVE** is sent by the device every 5 s while waiting, until REPLY_DONE, a failed
   RESULT, the next press or link loss. There is no time cap on either end (section 9).
8. **WORK**: `phase u8` (0 idle, 1 received, 2 thinking, 3 tool), then an optional
   UTF-8 label (always empty in v1).
9. **APP_STATE language** (protocol 1.2): one byte after `state`, the phone app's UI language,
   so the Passport shows its own text in the same language. `0` = Simplified Chinese (zh-Hans),
   `1` = English (en). The app sends it in every APP_STATE: at link ready, on every reachability
   change, and once more with the current state whenever its UI language changes. The device
   applies it at once (redraws its current screen) and keeps it across reboots. A missing byte
   (a 1.0/1.1 phone) or an unknown value leaves the device's language unchanged; the device's
   default is zh-Hans. Text that comes from the brain (RESULT text, REPLY, WORK label) is
   shown as sent and is not translated.

## 8. Phone behaviour

- INFO when the phone subscribes on the current connection, and again whenever the device
  receives APP_STATE (Passport firmware behaviour). The first covers a bonded reconnect: the
  device used to restore the subscription with the bond and send INFO before iOS had rediscovered
  the characteristic, so INFO was lost. The second covers an app relaunch on a live connection
  (state restoration, reinstall): the subscription never changes, but the app always sends
  APP_STATE after link ready, so INFO follows. A different `proto_major` is refused: the app
  sends APP_STATE 2, the Device page shows the mismatch, and press messages are ignored.
- One press = one voice note. AUDIO without a preceding PRESS_START (app relaunched mid-press)
  starts the press implicitly. A link drop mid-press uploads what arrived.
- RESULT after every press: 0 transcribed (with text), 1 empty, 2 asr_failed, 3 send_failed,
  4 not_connected_to_server. The upload's 202 is not the RESULT: the text is known only when the
  channel reports the message `cleaned` on `/live`, and RESULT is sent then (not on delivery, which
  can wait behind earlier room speech). The text is what the brain receives.

  | Event for the press's message | RESULT |
  |---|---|
  | `message cleaned` | 0, its text |
  | `failed(empty_transcript)` | 1 |
  | `failed(asr_failed)` | 2 |
  | `failed(perception_unavailable)`, or 503 `perception_unavailable` until the deadline | 3 |
  | transport failure until the deadline | 4 |
  | `failed(brain_unreachable)` after RESULT 0 | none; the reply wait ends with REPLY_DONE 0 |
  | reconnected before RESULT: not in `ready.messages`, its row replayed | 0, the row's text |
  | reconnected before RESULT: not in `ready.messages`, no row | 3 (the reason is not recoverable) |
  | `resultDeadline` passes without one of the above | 3, or 4 while `/live` is down |

  The deadline runs from PRESS_END and covers the upload too. The app registers the wait under the
  message id before uploading, so an event that beats the 202 is not lost.
- APP_STATE on link ready, whenever channel reachability changes, and when the UI language
  changes (item 9 of section 7).
- Reply correlation is by `message_ids`. The channel lists, on `answer_final`, `turn` and answer
  rows, the messages the turn covered: the input the brain anchored the turn on plus every input
  still open when the turn's first output arrived (the brain folds inputs sent while it works into
  the running turn). The reply to a press is the answer whose `message_ids` contains the press's
  message; it ends the wait. A message can appear in two turns (queued rather than folded); both
  are reported.
  - Live: that `answer_final`. Partial text is not forwarded while a message waits:
    `duoduo_said` names no message.
  - Room log: the answer row with that message in `message_ids`, from the replay before `ready`
    or `GET /api/imlog?since=`.
  - Any other answer arriving while a press waits is forwarded as usual (REPLY final +
    REPLY_DONE); the app keeps waiting for its message.
  - The same answer is sent once: an answer sent live is remembered until its row arrives (matched
    by text, the whole text for a cut answer, and `message_ids`); rows arrive once, above the
    cursor. A batch of rows (replay, fetch) sends only the answer naming the waited message, or
    else the newest answer.
  - Without a message id (KEEPALIVE adopted after a relaunch) the next answer is the reply.
    With no reply pending, every answer is forwarded.
- The brain can answer while the socket is down after a wake. That answer comes back as a row:
  replayed when the socket reopens from the cursor, or read with `GET /api/imlog?since=` at link
  ready and in Background App Refresh.
- KEEPALIVE wakes the app while a reply is pending: it re-checks the `/live` socket (a stale one is
  reopened from the cursor). KEEPALIVE received without a pending reply (after a relaunch) adopts
  the wait.

## 9. Reply wait and work status

| Event | Phone sends | Wait |
|---|---|---|
| `message cleaned` for the press (RESULT 0) | nothing more | starts |
| `turn` `received` whose `message_ids` contains the message | WORK 1 | continues |
| after that, `turn` `thinking` / `tool` | WORK 2 / 3 | continues |
| Other `turn` phases (`speaking`, `done`) | nothing | continues |
| Answer whose `message_ids` contains the message, live or from a row | REPLY final, REPLY_DONE id | ends |
| `turn` `idle` whose `message_ids` contains the message, no answer | WORK 0, REPLY_DONE 0 | ends |
| `message failed(brain_unreachable)` for the message | REPLY_DONE 0 | ends |
| `/live` cannot be reopened (dial or tsnet failure) | APP_STATE 1 (on change), REPLY_DONE 0 | ends |
| PRESS_START of a new press | nothing (the device ended its wait itself) | ends |
| Any other answer | REPLY final, REPLY_DONE id (when linked) | continues |

There is no time limit on the wait; a wait whose answer never comes ends through `turn idle`, the
socket, or a new press.
- Proactive answers: every answer the app receives is forwarded as REPLY
  final + REPLY_DONE, with or without a pending press, so the device can alert. The app receives
  answers live on `/live` (foreground, reply wait, ambient), as rows above its cursor when the
  link becomes ready (a BLE wake), and from Background App Refresh. Fully suspended, nothing
  arrives until the next wake. Without a pending press, partial REPLY texts (`final` 0) of a
  streaming answer precede the final one under the same id; a device that alerts should alert
  once per `reply_id`.
- When the link becomes ready the newest answer is sent only if the device was not handed it yet
  (it came while the link was down, possibly before a relaunch). Earlier builds re-sent the latest
  answer on every link-ready; with an alert on every REPLY that repeated the alert on each
  reconnect. The wire format is unchanged.
- Duplicates: an answer is forwarded once (see reply correlation). `reply.memory` also stores the
  newest answer and the newest final reply id handed to a ready link; a write without response lost
  in a link drop at that moment is not retried.
- The device side is unchanged by the move to messages: it waits for RESULT with keepalives and no
  timeout; only the meaning of the RESULT text (what the brain receives) and its later arrival
  changed. The link protocol stays 1.2.
